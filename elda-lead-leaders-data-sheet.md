### Notes

1. ELDA Lead Leaders develops leadership effectiveness through experiential learning conducted in challenging environments.
2. The selected adventure activity provides the experiential vehicle for leadership development; completion of the activity is not, in itself, the primary learning outcome.
3. Psychometric tools, self-evaluation, observation and feedback are used to increase learner self-awareness and understanding of their leadership behaviour and impact on others.
4. Learners are provided opportunities to deliberately select, apply and evaluate alternative leadership behaviours throughout the course.
5. Army ethos and values are to be demonstrated through observable leadership behaviour, particularly during periods of pressure, uncertainty and adversity.
6. Learning identified during ELDA Lead Leaders is to inform continued reflection, behavioural adaptation and deliberate leadership development beyond the course.
7. The Learning Provider is the New Zealand Army Leadership Centre (NZALC).
8. Instructor allocation and activity selection remain the responsibility of NZALC and are to comply with the NZALC Safety Management Plan and applicable activity SOPs.

## Course Structure

### COG 1: Adaptive Leadership Development

#### LO 1.1: Demonstrate Army ethos and values through leadership behaviour under challenging conditions

Learners demonstrate consistency between Army ethos and values and their observable leadership behaviour, particularly when operating under physical, cognitive and interpersonal pressure, uncertainty and adversity.

#### LO 1.2: Evaluate personal leadership behaviour and its impact on others

Learners use self-evaluation, psychometric insight, feedback and experiential observation to develop greater awareness of their leadership preferences and behaviours, and the impact those behaviours have on others.

#### LO 1.3: Adapt leadership behaviour to improve effectiveness

Learners deliberately select, apply and evaluate leadership behaviours appropriate to the situation, task and people being led in order to improve their leadership effectiveness.

#### LO 1.4: Develop an individual leadership development strategy

Learners identify leadership strengths and development priorities and establish deliberate behavioural strategies to support continued leadership development across subsequent appointments and contexts.

AI and Operational Effectiveness

How Army uses AI to increase operational effectiveness

A first-principles analysis for senior Army leadership  ·  Draft v0.1
The Question
How can Army use AI to increase operational effectiveness?
The Answer

Army should use AI where it relieves a material constraint on military output in ways that convert into capability.A small army that cannot fight when the tools are denied has not gained effectiveness. It has moved its dependence.

The Principles

1

Effect first

Adopted where it improves an Army output, and measured against it.

2

Task before tool

From the output and its constraint to the application, never the reverse.

3

The commander owns it

AI advises; it does not decide where consequence is high. Verify what you use. You own what you sign.

4

Assurance by consequence

More control where error costs more, lasts longer or is harder to check.

5

Competence before dependence

No one relies on a tool for a skill they must perform without it. Competence comes from practice against real tasks.

Priority Actions

1

Own it

Assign accountability for AI in Army; set risk appetite by consequence, so low-consequence use is enabled by default.

2

Prove effect in three places

Bounded, measured trials: planning and orders in a headquarters; training design in Army Training Group; readiness data. Stop what does not work.

3

Train it through the approach

AI competence built into individual training against real tasks, with tool-denied assurance of core skills. Instructors first.

4

Improve the data

Readiness and training data are likely to constrain the highest-value applications Army controls directly.

5

Compress the lessons cycle

From observation to updated training and tactics at a pace the force can act on.

# ALC Health & Safety Inspection

## Physical actions

* **C61 Q-Store:** Secure all unstable metal shelving/racking. Confirm rack load limits are displayed, heavy items are stored low, and high-level items are secured.
* **C61 Q-Store:** Remove loose items from the floor and improve storage so equipment is properly stowed.
* **C62 Main Store:** Fix unsecured cabinets to the wall/structure and remove boxes and ropes from the floor.
* **C62 Main Store:** Signpost the location of the first aid kit.
* **C62 Adventure Race Area:** Provide sufficient storage for equipment.
* **ALC Offices:** Secure all standalone cabinets and improve storage in the office adjacent to the kitchen.
* **ELDA Wing:** Tidy and secure loose cabling to remove trip hazards.

## H&S assurance

* Confirm all ALC staff have completed the required fire lecture within the last 12 months.
* Confirm all hazards identified above are captured on the appropriate Hazard Register.
* Confirm the current training status of ALC S&H representatives.
* Confirm all required ALC workplace/section inspections are current.

## Close-out

Once complete, record evidence of rectification and close the corresponding inspection actions.
